How to use it

Drag any player anywhere on the court. Rotating snaps every player to their zone's base position; dragging lets you test faults. The Libero can only swap in for a back-row player, and is automatically swapped out if that player would rotate to the front row. If the bench turns red, you've dragged its occupant onto a court that already has 6 players.

Libero Swap

Click "Swap In Libero", then click a highlighted back-row player to swap the Libero in for them.

Overlap Status

"Show Overlap Guides" and "Show Player Links" are independent toggles - while either is on, click a player to see its overlap boundaries and/or links to corresponding players. Double-click a player to lock the selection (pulses while locked) so you can move others without losing it; double-click again to unlock.

Player Names

Customize what each player's on-court label shows (e.g. a jersey number instead of the position code). Limited to 3 characters so it still fits inside the player circle. Saved labels apply next time the visualizer page loads.
